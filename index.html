How Let's Role is planned to work

A transparent process that respects both customers and sellers.

1

Post a project

Customers describe the work they need, their budget and how they prefer to be contacted.

2

Review a lead

Sellers see limited project details and a clearly displayed fee before choosing whether to unlock a lead.

3

Connect fairly

After verified payment and customer consent, the agreed contact details can be shared. Reviews should come from genuine interactions.
